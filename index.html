Our Mission at Robson Ranch

The Robson Ranch Pickleball Club facilitates the growth of pickleball across our Eloy, Arizona community—offering organized recreational and competitive play, free beginner instruction, and skill development in a collegial, sportsmanlike environment.

With 16 state-of-the-art courts (8 lighted until 10:00 PM) next to the Sports Club and 670 active members, the club also funds ongoing facility enhancements—including an approved HOA dinking wall and water/ice pavilion awaiting developer sign-off.

New Here & Our Story

Whether you are brand new to pickleball or bringing an established rating to Robson Ranch, here is how to get started, who leads the club, and how ratings work.

Three Steps to Your First Game

1
Pick Your Starting Path

Never played? Come to free Beginner Lessons Mondays or Wednesdays (8–10 AM, HOA Courts 1–2). Already rated? Join level play or bring a documented USAP, DUPR, or IPTPA rating for review.

2
Sign the HOA Waiver & Join

Sign the Robson Ranch HOA Liability Waiver at the Sports Desk (5750 N. Robson Blvd) and complete your club membership application. Dues are prorated by month ($30 Jan–Sep, $15 Oct, $10 Nov, $5 Dec).

3
Book Courts & Join Play Gizmo!

Use CourtReserve to reserve Courts 3–14 up to 3 days ahead (6:30 AM window), or register for weekly level-based Play Gizmo! sessions by Wednesday at 11:00 PM.

Skill Ratings & Level Advancement

RRAZ-PC ratings guide organized play from 2.0 Beginner through 4.5–5.0 Advanced (per Bylaws & Ratings Policy).

Testing & Transferring a Rating

Newcomers: Start at 2.0 (free beginner clinics) or request a placement review at rraz.pickleball.ratings@gmail.com.
Existing USAP / DUPR / IPTPA / APPL players: Email documented rating history to rrpbctreasure@gmail.com (no automatic self-rating transfers from unverified apps).
Testing Window: Club skills testing runs November 1 – March 31. Step-Up Testing requires 48+ points on the official skills assessment and verified match play.

Our Desert Pickleball Community

Nestled in the Santa Cruz Valley between Phoenix and Tucson, Robson Ranch has grown into one of Arizona's most active 55+ pickleball communities—now 670 members strong, with a $33,891 treasury balance and a 2027 budget approved 264–6.

More than 52 mentored players have graduated from our 2.5s & A Coach program this season, and our volunteer instructors keep free beginner lessons welcoming every Monday and Wednesday morning.

Join the Robson Ranch Pickleball Club

Robson Ranch residents and renters are welcome—whether you are picking up a paddle for the first time or competing in 4.5+ play.

Why Join RRPC?

Membership unlocks full access to club-reserved court blocks (Courts 3–14), weekly Play Gizmo! level play, CourtReserve Open Reservations, official skills ratings & Step-Up testing, two Playmate ball machines, socials, and the March In-House Tournament.

Every dollar of annual dues ($30 full year, prorated in fall) goes directly back into balls, equipment, clinics, and court improvements for our 670 members.
