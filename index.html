Welcome to the Courts

New Here?

No PDF scavenger hunt. Here’s how to join, take a free beginner class, get a club rating, and bring guests—all in plain English.

Step-by-Step

How to Join RRPC in 4 Easy Steps

1

Confirm Eligibility

Membership is open to all Robson Ranch homeowners, residents, and qualified tenants meeting HOA requirements. Non-residents are ineligible for membership but welcome as registered guests.

2

Choose Your Rating Path

No rating / new player? You start at 2.0 (Novice) and take our free Beginner Class to earn an initial 2.5–3.5 rating. Already rated? Bring a verifiable APPL two-digit rating or qualifying other-club rating within 15 days.

3

Complete the Form & HOA Waiver

Submit the Membership Form (online preview in our Membership tab, via CourtReserve, court drop-box, or email to rrpbctreasure@gmail.com) plus the HOA Waiver.

4

Pay Annual Dues & Jump In

Annual dues are $30/person (Jan–Sep), prorated to $15 in Oct, $10 in Nov, and $5 in Dec. Once active, reserve courts on CourtReserve or join rated round robins!

Free for Residents

Beginner Lessons

Offered year-round with no prior experience needed. Instructors cover rules, etiquette, safety, court attire, serving, scorekeeping, positioning, returns, and dinking—plus up to 6 round-robin games.

  • When: Mondays & Wednesdays, 8:00–10:00 AM
  • Where: HOA Courts 1 & 2
  • After Class: The Lead Beginner Instructor assigns your initial club rating (2.5, 3.0, or 3.5; most new players start at 2.5).

Ratings Policy · Oct 9, 2026

How Club Skill Ratings Work

Club ratings keep round-robin games competitive, safe, and fun for everyone.

2.0Novice / New to pickleball (before Beginner Class evaluation)
2.5Entry club level after Beginner Class; learning rules, scoring & rallies
3.0Consistent serves & returns; beginning dinks and third-shot drops
3.5Reliable soft game, court positioning, and strategic shot selection
4.0Fast-paced directional control, resets, and high consistency
4.5 / 5.0Advanced competitive & tournament-caliber play
What outside ratings are accepted when I join?

RRPC accepts the highest of: (1) a previous RRAZ-PC rating, (2) a qualifying rating from another club with a substantially similar numerical system (submit written proof within 15 days; provisional for 30 days), or (3) a verifiable two-digit APPL rating from the most recently concluded season.

Important: DUPR, IPTPA, UTR-P, observation-only ratings, and self-ratings are not accepted as an independent basis for a club rating.

How do I move up a rating level? (Testing: Nov 1 – Mar 31)
  1. Self-Assessment: Track scores from 4 gender-specific round robins at your current rating and complete the Rating Self-Assessment form on CourtReserve (“Edit the Copy”).
  2. Schedule Testing: Email rraz.pickleball.ratings@gmail.com (first-come, first-served; one initial test per season, one level at a time).
  3. STEP-UP Play: Once you have a confirmed test date, you may sign up on CourtReserve for up to 2 STEP-UP round robins at the next level (no walk-ons).
  4. 6-Game Control Test: Play 6 games to 11 (win by 1) with 6 Control Players. Score 48+ points to advance! Scoring 45–47 qualifies for one retest with an Outside Observer.
Can I request a temporary or permanent rating reduction?

Yes. Members may request a temporary reduction (medical reasons or temporary lack of competitiveness) or permanent reduction in writing to the Board / Treasurer (rrpbctreasure@gmail.com). Temporary reductions can be reinstated within 1 year; otherwise they become permanent.

Guest Policy · Effective Apr 3, 2021

Bringing Visiting Friends & Family

1. Register First

First-time guests go to the HOA Office (Ranch House front desk) with a guest guideline form, sign a fitness waiver, get a photo, and receive a guest pass. Arriving on a weekend? Register for a temporary pass at the Sports Club, then visit the HOA Office Monday morning.

2. Youth & Tenants

Guests under age 16 must have an adult in attendance on the courts. Qualified Tenants (renting a Robson Ranch home) are not guests—they join the club with a verified rating. Preferred Guests are treated the same as Club members.

3. Round-Robin Priority

Guests don’t pay club dues and play at their USAPA/IPTPA/club rating (or self-rate if unrated). When round robins are full, space priority goes: (a) Club members at that rating, (b) STEP-UP test candidates, (c) Club members playing down, (d) Registered Guests.

Pro Tip

Need Extra Players? Use “Is Open Reservation”

1. Start your booking in CourtReserve and enter the names of players already in your group (even if it’s just yourself!).
2. Check “Is Open Reservation” right on the reservation screen.
3. Pick play type & rating you’re looking for—CourtReserve automatically opens the remaining spots so fellow members can join your foursome.

Note: Reserved courts are considered abandoned 10 minutes after the top of the hour if players don’t show up, and become Open Play Paddle Up courts.

Effective Oct 23, 2026

HOA & Open Play Paddle Up

Applies to HOA Courts (1, 2, 15, 16) and any vacant club or abandoned reserved courts:

  1. Place your paddle in the rack in order of arrival.
  2. First 4 paddles take the court and play one game to 11.
  3. All 4 players rotate off after the game (unless no paddles are waiting).
  4. If only 2 paddles wait, those 2 may invite 2 players from the finished game to join; the other 2 place paddles at the back.
  5. Drills & Ball Machine: 20-minute limit when courts are full and players are paddled up (15-min timer starts when paddles go in rack per HOA sheet).

Updated Feb 5, 2025

Pickleball Ball Machine Checkout

Two Playmate ball machines are available to check out at the Sports Desk (5750 N. Robson Blvd · 520-426-3355) during normal hours.

  • Mandatory Training: Attend a club machine training session and bring the printed Checklist & HOA Waiver. The instructor signs off and the Sports Center uploads it to your profile.
  • Time Rules: 1-hour limit when others are waiting; cannot be checked out within 1½ hours of closing.
  • Safety: Never unjam the machine (call Sports Desk), wear protective eyewear, use the remote with toggle UP, and return the battery pack promptly.

Want to schedule machine training?

Email robsonranchpickleball@gmail.com or watch club announcements for the next group orientation.
