Player Testing & Rating Evaluations

How club skill ratings are assigned and advanced — including the November 1 – March 31 testing season, the Step-Up process, and a demo request form. Drawn from the official RRAZ-PC Ratings Policy (October 9, 2026).

How Testing Works

Club testing is voluntary and offered November 1 through March 31. Members may test upward only one rating level at a time (2.5 → 3.0 → 3.5 → 4.0 → 4.5).

1. Self-assessment first – Track scores from four gender-specific round robins at your current club rating and complete the Skills Self-Assessment for the rating you want (on CourtReserve under “Rating Self-Assessment”).
2. Request a test date – When you are ready, email the Testing Coordinator to be scheduled first-come, first-served.
3. Test format – One gender-specific six-game round robin with Control Players. Games are to 11, win by one (no rally scoring). A Court Monitor validates the scorecard.
4. The 48-point standard – At least 48 points over six games qualifies you to advance. A score of 45–47 allows one retest with an Outside Observer. Below 45, apply again in a later eligible season.

Who to Contact

Testing Coordinator (schedule a test or retest): rraz.pickleball.ratings@gmail.com
Treasurer (rating records, transfers, and roster updates): rrpbctreasure@gmail.com

The Step-Up Process

After your testing date is confirmed, you may play at the requested level to decide whether to continue with the scheduled test.

After a date is set

Limited Step-Up Play

You may join Step-Up play at the requested level for up to two round robins. Track your scores and then decide whether to take the scheduled advancement test.

CourtReserve only

Register — No Walk-Ons

Applicants must register on CourtReserve to participate in Step-Up. Walk-ons are not allowed. Missed assessments normally go to the end of the queue (reschedules possible for illness or emergency with two hours’ notice).

New members

Incoming Ratings

Residents with a verifiable prior RRAZ-PC, qualifying other-club (written proof within 15 days; provisional 30 days), or qualifying APPL rating start at the highest of those. DUPR, IPTPA, UTR-P, and self-ratings are not accepted on their own. No verified rating? You start at Novice 2.0 and attend free Beginner Classes (Mon/Wed 8–10 AM, HOA Courts 1–2).

Membership & Renewal

The Robson Ranch Pickleball Club is a private club for Robson Ranch homeowners, residents, and qualified tenants only. Non-residents are ineligible for membership (Bylaws, Articles VI–VII).

Renew for the Coming Year

Annual dues are payable by January 31. Members in good standing who are current on dues may vote and take part in all club events.
